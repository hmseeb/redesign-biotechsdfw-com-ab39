Privacy Policy

How BioTechs Crime & Trauma Scene Cleaning handles the information you share with us through this website.

What we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send us. When you submit a quote request or contact form, that includes your name, phone number, email address if you provide one, the location and service you are asking about, and whatever details you type into the message field. Our forms also record which page you submitted from so we can return you to it.

How we use it

Your information is used to respond to your request, schedule and perform work, and follow up about that work. Given the nature of our services, we treat every enquiry as confidential.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share the details of your situation with anyone who is not involved in responding to it, except where we are required to by law.

Form processing

Form submissions on this website are delivered to us through a form-handling service that passes the message to our team. The data is used only to deliver your enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not run third-party tracking scripts. Pages you open from this site on third-party platforms (Google Maps, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn, Tumblr, Flickr) are governed by those platforms’ own privacy policies.

Retention

Enquiries and job records are kept only as long as needed to provide service and to meet our business and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email jesse@biotechsdfw.com or call (817) 270-6363 and we will take care of it.

Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
