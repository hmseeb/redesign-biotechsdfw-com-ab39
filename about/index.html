About us

Quiet, careful work when it matters most

A crime, suicide, unattended death or serious biohazard leaves behind more than a mess: it leaves a property that is unsafe to occupy and a family or business that has to keep functioning anyway.

BioTechs exists to take that part off your plate. Our technicians assess the affected area, contain it, remove and properly dispose of biohazardous material, then clean, disinfect and deodorize so the space can be used again. We explain the scope before we start, work discreetly, and keep what we see to ourselves.

We are available around the clock, every day of the year, providing fast, professional response whenever you need support restoring your space — from our Fort Worth office on Ray White Road and our Dallas office on Noel Road.

What makes a BioTechs job different

  • Immediate response. 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays.
  • Discretion. Low-key arrivals and no explanations owed to neighbors, tenants or coworkers.
  • Containment-first method. The affected area is isolated so contamination does not spread through the property.
  • Proper disposal. Biohazardous material is removed and disposed of the way regulated waste should be.
  • Finish work. Odor treatment, disinfection and sanitizing so the space is genuinely usable, not just visually clean.
  • BBB Accredited Business. Accountability you can check before you let a crew in the door.

BioTechs is a BBB Accredited Business serving Fort Worth and Dallas, Texas.
