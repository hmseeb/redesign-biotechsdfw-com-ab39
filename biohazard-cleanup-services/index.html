Our process

How a biohazard job runs

  1. Assess

    We evaluate the scene, identify every contaminated surface and material, and explain the scope of work.

  2. Contain

    The work zone is isolated to stop contamination and odor from spreading into unaffected areas.

  3. Remove & disinfect

    Biohazardous material is removed and disposed of properly; remaining surfaces are cleaned and disinfected.

  4. Deodorize & verify

    Odor treatment and a final walkthrough confirm the space is ready to be used again.
