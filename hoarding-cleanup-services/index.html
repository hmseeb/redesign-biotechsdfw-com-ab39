What to expect

A hoarding cleanup, step by step

  1. Walkthrough

    We assess volume, structural concerns, biohazards and access, then build a realistic plan and quote.

  2. Sort & salvage

    Keep, donate and discard decisions stay with the client or family. Valuables and documents are set aside, not thrown out.

  3. Removal & haul-off

    Material is removed from the property and disposed of appropriately, including any biohazardous waste.

  4. Clean & deodorize

    Surfaces are cleaned, disinfected and treated for odor so the home is genuinely usable again.
